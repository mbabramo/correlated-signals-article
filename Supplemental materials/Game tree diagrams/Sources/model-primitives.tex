\toprule Primitive & Core specifications \\ \midrule
Case quality & Unobserved continuous quality $Q\sim U[0,1]$ \\
True liability & $T=1$ means truly liable. Given quality $q$, $\Pr(T=1\mid Q=q)=q$; the unconditional probability is $\Pr(T=1)=0.5$. \\
Signal generation & Each party and the court receive a noisy assessment drawn from a normal distribution centered on $q$, truncated to $[0,1]$. Assessments are independent conditional on exact $q$. \\
Signal noise & $\sigma_P=\sigma_D=\sigma_C=0.20$ \\
Party signals & $X_P\in\mathcal{S}_P$ and $X_D\in\mathcal{S}_D$, with $|\mathcal{S}_P|=|\mathcal{S}_D|=10$. Ten equal-width intervals per party have midpoint labels $0.05,0.15,\ldots,0.95$. \\
Court signal and rule & $X_C\in\mathcal{S}_C$, with $|\mathcal{S}_C|=2$. The court finds no liability for assessments below $0.5$, and liability for assessments at or above $0.5$. \\
Damages & Fixed award $d=1$ if P wins at trial or D defaults; zero if P abandons or loses at trial \\
Initial wealth & $w_P=w_D=10$ \\
Filing and answering & P chooses whether to file; after filing, D chooses whether to answer or default. Nonfiling ends the dispute without payment or litigation costs. \\
Bargaining rounds & Single simultaneous round \\
Offer grid & $\mathcal{O}=\{0.05,0.15,\ldots,0.95\}$, with $|\mathcal{O}|=10$ choices per party. The finer-grid check uses $|\mathcal{O}|=15$ midpoint offers with the same signal sets. \\
Settlement rule & Settlement occurs if P's demand is no greater than D's offer; D pays P the midpoint of the two amounts. \\
Exit & Before offers, each party privately commits to continue or quit if bargaining fails. P may abandon; D may default. Trial occurs only if bargaining fails and neither quits. \\
Mutual exit & If both commit to quit and bargaining fails, a lottery selects P's abandonment or D's default with probability $0.5$ each. \\
Stage costs & $c_{file}=c_{answer}=c_{trial}=0.15$ at ordinary costs; trial costs are additional and per party. Each incurs $0.30$ through trial before fee shifting. No separate bargaining cost. \\
Cost multipliers & A common multiplier $c\in\{0.25,0.5,1,2,4\}$ scales all stage costs; ordinary costs use $c=1$. \\
Preferences & Both parties maximize expected utility: risk neutral, $U(W)=W$, or CARA, $U(W)=-e^{-\alpha W}$ with $\alpha=2$ for both parties. \\
