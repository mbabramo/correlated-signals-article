{\LARGE\bfseries Welfare decompositions: rule and behavior}\par\medskip
36 of 36 American/British pairs are available. Each pair uses independently audited full profiles, including agreement and off-path actions. Alternative truth maps are not included.\par\smallskip
Write $W_{rs}$ for welfare evaluated under rule $r$ with the complete profile solved under rule $s$; $A$ denotes American and $C$ denotes British. The mixed corners are evaluations of fixed profiles, not new equilibria. The effects average the two replacement orders:\par
\[R=\tfrac12[(W_{CA}-W_{AA})+(W_{CC}-W_{AC})],\qquad B=\tfrac12[(W_{AC}-W_{AA})+(W_{CC}-W_{CA})].\]
The signed total is $\Delta=W_{CC}-W_{AA}=R+B$. No equilibrium-selection inference follows from this accounting decomposition. Five separate measures retain their existing definitions.\par\medskip
