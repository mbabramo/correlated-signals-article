# Baseline costs and dispositions

`disposition.pdf` is the assembled Figure C1 candidate: the five saved cost
levels under the American rule and trial-contingent fee shifting, with risk
neutrality and voluntary filing, answering, and later exit. All seven terminal
categories use all potential disputes. The label British is preserved from the
CS004 source data; its fee liability is triggered at trial.

The figure and its caption, exact-data JSON, TeX, and PNG preview were generated
from `baseline-costs.request.json`. No equilibrium was recomputed. Regenerate it
through `diagrams dispositions --config article-diagrams.json` in LitigCharts.

The different fee trigger is compared separately in `../Fee shifting on exit`.
