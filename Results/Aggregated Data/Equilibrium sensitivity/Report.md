# Equilibrium tremble sensitivity

203 profiles and 6496 unilateral response checks. The original complete policies, including off-path actions, were retained. Opponent trembles mix each saved policy with uniform or fixed positive distributions; chance and own pre-response policies remain unchanged.

[Per-profile statistics](profiles.csv) · [Every check](all-tremble-tests.csv) · [Protocol and validation](validation-and-summary.json)

Gains use the full terminal utility range. Behavior distances compare with the zero-tremble selected response, with original-reach and perturbed-reach weights reported separately. Tie selection retains original probabilities within the existing 1e-10 threshold and checks full regret at 1e-8. These are local response diagnostics, not new equilibria, a perfection certificate, or selection probabilities.
